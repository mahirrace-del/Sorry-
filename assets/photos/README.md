# Photo Organization Guide — Plug & Play Photos for the Memory Book

Drop your real photos into the folders below. You can drop multiple images into each folder, and we will place them gracefully across the story scenes, polaroid frames, and the Chapter X Memory Book spread!

---

### Folder Directory & What to Put in Each:

1. **`01-school-and-classmates/`**
   * *Type*: School-era memories, online classes, old school campus, or batch photos.
   * *Used in*: Chapter 1 (Classmates).

2. **`02-science-project-and-crafts/`**
   * *Type*: Series/parallel circuit project, tiny hand-painted paper sofa and bed, craft work, or initial Messenger chats.
   * *Used in*: Chapter 2 (The Paper House).

3. **`03-rooftops-and-music/`**
   * *Type*: Singing on the rooftop, guitar playing, evening skies, or balcony snap views.
   * *Used in*: Chapter 3 (Across the Rooftops).

4. **`04-coaching-and-street-walks/`**
   * *Type*: Coaching center memories, evening street walks, fuchka/snack cart moments.
   * *Used in*: Chapter 4 (Our Own Little Language).

5. **`05-river-and-sunset-dates/`**
   * *Type*: River boat ride, riverbank sunset, water reflections, and rickshaw ride back.
   * *Used in*: Chapter 5 (River Sunset).

6. **`06-dates-and-outings/`**
   * *Type*: Favorite cafe dates, restaurant outings, parks, or walks together.
   * *Used in*: Memory Book & Chapter Frames.

7. **`07-fun-and-silly-moments/`**
   * *Type*: Playful funny selfies, goofy laughter, candid unposed moments.
   * *Used in*: Memory Book & Story Highlights.

8. **`08-chocolates-and-little-gifts/`**
   * *Type*: The framed chocolate wrapper photo, flowers, treats, or sweet little keepsakes.
   * *Used in*: Chapter 8 (The Framed Wrapper) & Memory Book.

9. **`09-late-night-calls-and-chats/`**
   * *Type*: Late-night call screen, memorable chat screenshots, November 2023 memories.
   * *Used in*: Chapter 9 (November Night).

10. **`10-letters-and-keepsakes/`**
    * *Type*: Handwritten letters, cute notes, cards, or handmade paper memories.
    * *Used in*: Chapter 10 (Memory Book Spread letter slots).

11. **`11-random-memories/`**
    * *Type*: Any everyday favorite memories, candid pictures, or random photos you love.
    * *Used in*: Scattered throughout the Memory Book & ending scenes.

---

### Supported Formats:
* `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`
* You can put as many pictures as you want in each folder! Once you drop them in or share them, we will link them directly to each scene and polaroid frame in the story.
